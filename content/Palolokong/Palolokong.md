The scattered Islands that make up the Archipelago in the ocean of Kra are collectively known as [[Palolokong (Deity) | Palolokong]]. Its name comes from the myth of the winged serpent bearing the same name and was chosen by the early inhabitants of the land.

Splintering from the primary continent of [[Mor-Thir]] after the [[Cataclysm]] brought about by the [[First People]] approximately 50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS[^1] the various islands that make up the archipelago drifted across the ocean to eventually settle on the opposite side of the planet. As part of the [[Second Awakening]] the [[Realm of Shadow]] was interposed between the material plane closest to Palolokong and the [[Fae Realm]] which was placed nearest to Mor-Thir.

Unlike its sister land mass Palolokong did not become populated by denizens of the highly [[magic]] infused plane of existence that buffered it from the chaos of the plane of magic made by the First People during the [[First Awakening]]. This meant that the inhabitants of the archipelago were uniquely human with only occasional instances of [[Fae touched]] lineages appearing across the islands. The second primary difference between Palolokong and Mor-Thir is the existence of [[Ur-Dieties]] that do not rely on the faith of their followers for material existence. Lastly Palolokong, as compared with Mor-Thir, has access to wellsprings of raw magic but does not have a miasmic permeation of magic across the land.
### History

##### PreHistory 
*(Dawn of existence through 100 Ma pPS)*

Prior to the First Awakening the [[Faces of the Cosmos]] moved the chaos of existence into varying degrees of order outside of time until at a certain point some pieces of the Cosmos left behind formed into a plane of existence which held within it a universe and within that galaxies and within one of those galaxies a planet. This planet bore a singular immense land mass that would come to be known as Mor-Thir but at the time of its inception held no name. This nameless landmass witnessed the shaping of a grain of the Cosmos into the First People, which bore resemblance to one of the many faces of their forbears and is known as the First Awakening.

##### First Awakening
*100 Ma BPS through 50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS*

The First People found the pieces of the Cosmos lingering in their environment and brought them together to form a miasmic plane of magic overlaid onto their Universe which allowed for quick access to generative powers. The establishment of the plane of magic brought about the Cataclysm and fractured the singular land mass of Mor-Thir into six large continents, severing the sea floor connection with five of the six causing them to float slowly across the ocean currents to settle in a magnetic ring on the opposite side of the planet. Within and around these collective continents known as Palolokong small islands formed through various plate tectonic shifts and came to exist as an established archipelago.

The generative magical energy left in the wake of the Cataclysm formed into the Fae Realm and the Realm of Shadow which through their interposition between, apart, and of the universe that holds Mor-Thir and Palolokong. The formation of these rich magic planes exist because of and brought on the Second Awakening which provided life as it is understood now to the planet, the galaxy, and beyond.

##### Second Awakening
*50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS through 0 pPS *

Though documented history in Palolokong only begins some 0.01 Ma years pPS historical records dating back some 10 Ma years pPS exist in the halls of shrewd Devils in the Realm of Shadow. After the Second Awakening created the Realms of Fae and Shadow it took a significant amount of time for the roiling magical energies to stabilize and allow space for life to exist. Existing in duality to one another the Fae Realm took on a more orderly role to the chaos of its sibling and though magic stabilized enough to form the two distinct [[Fiendish]] lineages of [[Devil]] and [[Demon]] the underlying chaos of the Realm of Shadow colored these new creations and informed their behavior significantly. With a tendency towards chaotic behavior the Devils and Demons found that their interest lay not in migration towards Palolokong but rather on internal conflict.

Preoccupation with internal affairs kept the Fiends of the Realm of Shadow unaware of life on Palolokong for millions of years until approximately 4 to 3 Ma years pPS when [[Humanoids]] first became recognized by the Ur-Dieties as potential threats. The acknowledgement by the Ur-Dieites signaled to the Fiends of the Realm of Shadow that there were creatures on the material plane capable of being influenced that might expand the ease of access between the Realm of Shadow and the material plane. As it stood, the lack of miasmic web of magic across Palolokong and the guarding of magic [[Wellsprings]] by Ur-Dieties meant that Fiends had extremely limited access to the material plane. Humanoids, capable of influence, could be contacted and brought into contracts with Fiends that wished to move operations into the material plane.

Slow interactions between Humanoids and Fiends occurred alongside Ur-Diety influence on burgeoning Humanoid civilizations and the birth of [[Nu-Dieties]] formed from collective belief. From 1 Ma to ~0.3 Ma years pPS the three intense magical forces shaped the Humanoid populations of Palolokong leading to the formation of the [[High Kingdoms]] on the outer islands of the archipelago.

Written records by and of Humanoid civilizations date as far back as 0.01 Ma years pPS and continue through to the [[Planar Schism]] detailing the complicated history of the High Kingdoms, the [[Mechanist Wars of Independence]] beginning in the year 1242 pPS, the formation of the [[Great Green]] and the [[Weather Society]] in 1001 pPS, the [[Last Wars of the Kingdoms]] beginning in 871 pPS and the [[Age of Peace]] closing out the remaining 300 years pre Planar Schism.
##### Planar Schism

The events leading up to the Planar Schism are not known in the histories of Palolokong given that it came from happenings on the other side of the world. Little is known even of a Pre Planar Schism Palolokong by those in the current era as most written records kept in the libraries of the Kingdom [[Sankovic-Chateaudun]], the Great Green, and the archives of the [[Mechanists]] were either destroyed or lost.
