The scattered Islands that make up the Archipelago in the ocean of Kra are collectively known as [[Palolokong (Deity) | Palolokong]]. Its name comes from the myth of the winged serpent bearing the same name and was chosen by the early inhabitants of the land.

Splintering from the primary continent of [[Mor-Thir]] after the [[Cataclysm]] brought about by the [[First People]] approximately 50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS[^1] the various islands that make up the archipelago drifted across the ocean to eventually settle on the opposite side of the planet. As part of the [[Second Awakening]] the [[Realm of Shadow]] was interposed between the material plane closest to Palolokong and the [[Fae Realm]] which was placed nearest to Mor-Thir.

Unlike its sister land mass Palolokong did not become populated by denizens of the highly [[magic]] infused plane of existence that buffered it from the chaos of the plane of magic made by the First People during the [[First Awakening]]. This meant that the inhabitants of the archipelago were uniquely human with only occasional instances of [[Fae touched]] lineages appearing across the islands. The second primary difference between Palolokong and Mor-Thir is the existence of [[Ur-Dieties]] that do not rely on the faith of their followers for material existence. Lastly Palolokong, as compared with Mor-Thir, has access to wellsprings of raw magic but does not have a miasmic permeation of magic across the land.
### History

##### PreHistory 
*(Dawn of existence through 100 Ma pPS)*

Prior to the First Awakening the [[Faces of the Cosmos]] moved the chaos of existence into varying degrees of order outside of time until at a certain point some pieces of the Cosmos left behind formed into a plane of existence which held within it a universe and within that galaxies and within one of those galaxies a planet. This planet bore a singular immense land mass that would come to be known as Mor-Thir but at the time of its inception held no name. This nameless landmass witnessed the shaping of a grain of the Cosmos into the First People, which bore resemblance to one of the many faces of their forbears and is known as the First Awakening.

##### First Awakening
*100 Ma BPS through 50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS*

The First People found the pieces of the Cosmos lingering in their environment and brought them together to form a miasmic plane of magic overlaid onto their Universe which allowed for quick access to generative powers. The establishment of the plane of magic brought about the Cataclysm and fractured the singular land mass of Mor-Thir into six large continents, severing the sea floor connection with five of the six causing them to float slowly across the ocean currents to settle in a magnetic ring on the opposite side of the planet. Within and around these collective continents known as Palolokong small islands formed through various plate tectonic shifts and came to exist as an established archipelago.

The generative magical energy left in the wake of the Cataclysm formed into the Fae Realm and the Realm of Shadow which through their interposition between, apart, and of the universe that holds Mor-Thir and Palolokong. The formation of these rich magic planes exist because of and brought on the Second Awakening which provided life as it is understood now to the planet, the galaxy, and beyond.

##### Second Awakening
*50 ± 0.2 Ma pPS through 0 pPS *

Though documented history in Palolokong only begins some 0.01 Ma years pPS historical records dating back some 10 Ma years pPS exist in the halls of shrewd Devils in the Realm of Shadow. After the Second Awakening created the Realms of Fae and Shadow it took a significant amount of time for the roiling magical energies to stabilize and allow space for life to exist. Existing in duality to one another the Fae Realm took on a more orderly role to the chaos of its sibling and though magic stabilized enough to form the two distinct [[Fiendish]] lineages of [[Devil]] and [[Demon]] the underlying chaos of the Realm of Shadow colored these new creations and informed their behavior significantly. With a tendency towards chaotic behavior the Devils and Demons found that their interest lay not in migration towards Palolokong but rather on internal conflict.

Preoccupation with internal affairs kept the Fiends of the Realm of Shadow unaware of life on Palolokong for millions of years until approximately 4 to 3 Ma years pPS when [[Humanoids]] first became recognized by the Ur-Dieties as potential threats. The acknowledgement by the Ur-Dieites signaled to the Fiends of the Realm of Shadow that there were creatures on the material plane capable of being influenced that might expand the ease of access between the Realm of Shadow and the material plane. As it stood, the lack of miasmic web of magic across Palolokong and the guarding of magic [[Wellsprings]] by Ur-Dieties meant that Fiends had extremely limited access to the material plane. Humanoids, capable of influence, could be contacted and brought into contracts with Fiends that wished to move operations into the material plane.

Slow interactions between Humanoids and Fiends occurred alongside Ur-Diety influence on burgeoning Humanoid civilizations and the birth of [[Nu-Dieties]] formed from collective belief. From 1 Ma to ~0.3 Ma years pPS the three intense magical forces shaped the Humanoid populations of Palolokong leading to the formation of the [[High Kingdoms]] on the outer islands of the archipelago.

Written records by and of Humanoid civilizations date as far back as 0.01 Ma years pPS and continue through to the [[Planar Schism]] detailing the complicated history of the High Kingdoms, the [[Mechanist Wars of Independence]] beginning in the year 1242 pPS, the formation of the [[Great Green]] and the [[Weather Society]] in 1001 pPS, the [[Last Wars of the Kingdoms]] beginning in 871 pPS and the [[Age of Peace]] closing out the remaining 500 years pre Planar Schism.
##### Planar Schism

The War of Fae and Shadow in Mor-Thir ended with the Planar Schism which delineates the current era and the past. King Oberon and Queen Titania of the Fae Realm in their efforts to bar denizens of the Realm of Shadow from leaving their home plane instead split the planet in two, linking the two halves of the Material Plane with their respective foils (Fae Realm and Realm of Shadow) with only the smallest of tethers from the plane of magic and fully breaking contact between Mor-Thir and Palolokong.

Breaking the world apart had significant implications for both Mor-Thir and Palolokong. (For information regarding a Post Planar Schism Mor-Thir please look to it's page) After the closing of the Last Wars of the Kingdoms the Age of Peace in Palolokong was marked by a significant boom in magical technology. The Great Green, having convinced the [[Mechanists]] to join their fight against the combined forces of the Kingdom [[Sankovic-Chateaudun]] and the Realm of Shadow, found that their tech echoed that of the Mechanists and together the two nations created a post-scarcity world.

Having been in a long Age of Peace, Palolokong was either negatively or positively affected by the Planar Schism. Druidic keepers of the magic wells throughout the archipelago find the move away from long established ritual towards magic technology an affront to the natural world; therefore the Planar Schism and the destruction of established nations was welcome. The opposite view holds that the Post Planar Schism world is a return to a scarcity mindset and an end to the age of enlightenment and all the potential it held. All view points however rely primarily on interpretations of oral accounts from those that remembered a Pre-Planar Schism world and chose to write down their experiences. The lack of information on what Palolokong was like prior to the schism, or even what caused the schism, is due to the fact the most written records kept in the libraries of the Kingdom Sankovic-Chateaudun and the Great Green along with the archives of the Mechanists were either destroyed or lost.

##### Post Planar Schism (PPS)

The Current age of Palolokong is distinctly low magic and low tech. Despite the transition back to a scarcity society the small city-states that formed in the vacuum left from the dissolution of the singular government of the Great Green exist in a distinctly communal manner. Refurbished [[arcano-tech]] is often blended with low tech to support regenerative agriculture and infrastructure. A significant population exist outside of the city-states however and are only able to be loosely supported by the larger communities; focusing instead on building up singular homesteads or small villages/towns of five to fifty families.

As a whole the current age of Palolokong is still one of peace despite the significant slide back in information technology from the prior era.

### Geography

Palolokong's geography is highly varied. Made up of five primary super-islands, bordering on continents, that surround the many smaller islands of the archipelago it ranges from Alpine climates in the south to tropics in the interior and tropical highlands in the north. 

The super-islands to the west, south, and south east have large mountain ranges that follow the curve of the land and block cold ocean winds from entering into the center of the archipelago. The narrowing of colder ocean currents into the spaces between the southerly super islands means that the interior and northern islands are primarily tropical with distinct dry and wet seasons that coincide with the mixing of warm and cold ocean currents from the north and south respectively meeting in the middle of the archipelago and creating heavy rainfall.

Despite the tropical interior of the archipelago, the inner side of the southerly super-islands are marked by hot dry summers and cool wet winters making them a favorable agricultural climate for grains. Moving from the inside out, the climate of the southern super-islands transitions to a temperate rainforest on the inner facing mountainsides and shifts to an alpine climate on the exterior mountain face. The cold dry alpine climate of the ocean facing mountains of the super-islands makes it a prime place for growing juniper orchards that the people of Palolokong harvest the berries of to turn into a variety of Juniper based alchohol products.

### Politics

### Economy

Taking queues from the Age of Peace, the current economic system in Palolokong primarily functions around a loose monetary system of "individuated worth" where goods and services are paid for with pebs based on the buyers sense of personal value rather than the sellers. Pebs exist 'digitally' within the aggregate city states throughout the archipelago and are converted to physical tokens or vice versa whensoever needed. 

Outside of the city states the systems of "individuated worth" still largely hold, but are mixed with barter and favor based trade systems. This blend of economic systems presents some challenges but as a whole functions fairly well and keeps the larger cultural values of inter-dependence intact.

### Demographics

Prior to the Planar Schism Palolokong was predominately populated by humans with almost no fae-touched ethnic lineages present in the archipelago. This did not mean that there was not a diversity of lineages and several unique ethnic groups existed including the undersea amoebic slimes, various humanoid animal groups, the Mechanists, the moth riders of the south west, and many more.

After the Planar Schism some native Palolokong lineages were no longer found in the archipelago and a large influx of fae-touched ethnic groups suddenly appearing from Mor-Thir. Though the primary population is still largely human Palolokong has increased in ethinc and cultural diversity creating a variety of challenges and opportunities.
